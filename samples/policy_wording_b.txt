AROGYA PLUS FAMILY FLOATER - TERMS AND CONDITIONS (FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR HELD-OUT TESTING)
Written for AppealPilot's held-out eval. Wording and numbering deliberately differ from the main sample.

Section 1. What this policy covers
1.1 We will reimburse reasonable and customary charges for in-patient treatment when a covered member is admitted to a hospital on the written advice of a doctor for at least one full day and night.
1.2 Procedures that modern medicine performs in under a day, such as cataract operations, dialysis, radiotherapy and endoscopic stone removal, are paid as day procedures without the one-day admission rule.
1.3 Ambulance charges up to Rs 2,000 per admission are payable.

Section 2. When cover begins
2.1 No claim is payable for an illness first appearing within one month of the start of cover with us, unless it results from an accident or unless the member held health cover with any insurer for a year or more immediately before joining us.
2.2 Conditions the member already had, which a doctor diagnosed or treated within four years before cover began, are covered only after four years of uninterrupted membership.
2.3 Named treatments (hernia, cataract, joint replacement, kidney stones, gall bladder removal) are covered after two years of uninterrupted membership, except where needed because of an accident.
2.4 Time spent insured with a previous insurer, without a gap, counts towards every waiting time in this Section.

Section 3. What we do not pay for
3.1 Treatment for weight loss, cosmetic changes to appearance, and treatment for substance addiction.
3.2 Stays arranged only for tests where no active treatment follows. Tests that lead to a diagnosis treated in the same stay are paid.
3.3 Treatment outside India.

Section 4. Room charges
4.1 Room charges are paid up to Rs 5,000 per day. If a member chooses a more expensive room, the doctor, surgeon and nursing fees are paid in the same ratio as the room charge we pay bears to the room charge billed.
4.2 The ratio in 4.1 is never applied to medicines, surgical consumables, implants, laboratory tests or intensive care charges.

Section 5. Making a claim
5.1 Tell us within two days of an emergency admission.
5.2 Send your papers within one month of leaving hospital.
5.3 We will accept late notice or late papers where you had a good reason, and we will not refuse a claim only because notice was late.
5.4 If we decline a claim we will write to you giving the exact term of this policy we relied on.

Section 6. Honesty and the five-year rule
6.1 If a member gives us false or incomplete information on the application, we may cancel cover.
6.2 Once a member has been insured with us (or with an insurer they moved from) for five years in a row, we will not refuse a claim or cancel cover because of incorrect or incomplete information on the application, unless we prove fraud.
